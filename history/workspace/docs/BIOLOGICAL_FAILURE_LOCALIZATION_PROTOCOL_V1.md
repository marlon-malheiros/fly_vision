## Source-based rationale

The [Erginkaya paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-025-01948-9)
reports little bIPS response to contralateral back-to-front motion despite H2
input, and discusses local inhibition/gating. The original chemical model lacks
those recurrent families. Existing expanded anatomy has 25 qualified family
candidates, with known homology and missing-electrical-synapse limits.

The [author analysis](https://github.com/ChiappeLab/Erginkaya_et_al_2025)
normalizes the two displayed motion epochs using their respective preceding
baselines. Its signed formula differs from the magnitude formula used in our
last benchmark. These are comparison differences to quantify, not reasons to
rewrite the prior metric. Raw baseline variation and local neurite observation
mapping remain measurement constraints.

## Measurement and statistics

Retain inherited calibration/test animal assignments and all eight separate
reporter/compartment/background cohorts; the four later bIPS walking-prep
animals remain a separately named, now-inspected group. Author soma metadata
is primary. An opposite-side counterfactual is diagnostic only unless anatomical
provenance independently establishes a mapping error. Never choose sides by fit.

Use all saved raw selected trial/ROI arrays and the existing quality rules.
Evaluate three measurement summaries without selecting the favorable one:

- `global_first`: the original first-baseline normalization.
- `local_common_scale`: center each motion epoch on its own preceding
  1--2-s or 8--9-s baseline, but retain its first-baseline SD. This isolates drift.
- `local_z`: center and scale each epoch by its own measured baseline.

The source local-z procedure is independently approximated on the actual saved
recording clocks, rather than called an exact rerun of the author's resampling
and session pooling. Keep per-trial baselines, standard deviations and exclusions.
Require both relevant baseline SDs finite/resolved for paired normalization;
report changing unit counts rather than quietly substituting first-baseline SD.
Do not invent model baseline noise. For predictions, subtract the filtered
model's local baseline and use the measured first/second SD ratio to transfer
first-baseline model units to local-z units. These baseline measurements are
observed normalization covariates, not fitted neural noise or outcome tuning.

Compute last-500-ms epoch contrasts. Magnitude DI is the original absolute
formula. Source-signed DI uses translation second-minus-first and yaw
second-minus-first for a left soma; right-soma yaw reverses order. H2 has the
opposite sign for both, following the author code; symmetric translation does
not flip with soma side. Keep raw components and undefined zero denominators.
Average within fly before cohort summaries. Use descriptive 95% fly-bootstrap
intervals, 5,000 draws with fixed seed; no test outcome selects a metric.

Odd/even trial-index halves give repeatability diagnostics using the same
measurement summaries and all qualifying ROIs. Each half needs at least two
distinct valid trials per condition; retain unavailable halves. Report sign
agreement and correlation, not a new biological acceptance cutoff.

Compare all six graph variants and two conditional references with the same
V1 observation filters/gains as the primary transfer mode. Secondary own-model
fits may use only inherited calibration animals' bilateral global_first traces,
all 56 existing candidates, equal-fly MSE and deterministic tie order. Freeze
all such fits before reused test/unilateral/walking-prep scoring. These are
diagnostic comparisons, not fresh calibration/validation after model selection.
Waveform scoring remains in the original global_first units; local-normalization
analyses concern the explicitly transformed epoch indices.

Report paired changes in both selectivity and zero-response waveform skill,
including unilateral transfer. A candidate explanation is supported within
the model only when the relevant controlled intervention changes the predicted
quantity; this is not biological causality. A favorable metric or lesion does
not overturn the original frozen failure. Do not select a replacement model.
