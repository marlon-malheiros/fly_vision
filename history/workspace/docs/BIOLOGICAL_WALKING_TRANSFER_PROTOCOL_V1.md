# Independent-animal bIPS transfer supplement V1

Specified 27 October 2026 from metadata only, during the frozen main benchmark
and before measurement fitting or new animal responses were inspected. This
supplement does not modify the main protocol, acceptance rules, splits or data.

The complete author-archive path inventory contains six additional intact
native-family fly identities beyond the main eligible set. Metadata identifies
five bIPS/sytGC7f/axon flies with `prepType=walking`. Four have the same 13-s
yaw and YawRightFlipped conditions; three also have unilateral conditions. The
remaining fly's matching motion conditions occur only in six-second speed
protocols and is ineligible here. One additional GC6f WT control has only
six-second conditions and is also ineligible. Exclude neither an eligible
animal nor a condition based on its measured response.

Verify the four eligible identities are absent from the prior observed-trace
and speed-condition analyses and from every main calibration/test assignment.
The public paper and archive metadata were inspected, but their response arrays
were not used in our earlier numerical comparisons. Preserve this exposure
definition; do not claim independent acquisition by our project.

Use all eligible 13-s native bIPS/axon/sytGC7f sessions, actual TimeAxis, author
ROIs, measured 1--2-s trial/ROI normalization and the main quality rules.
Preserve exact genotype strings and report the two genotype strata separately
in individual-animal results. The combined four-fly comparison concerns this
source-defined reporter/compartment set, not all bIPS genotypes.

Transfer the main immobilized bIPS/sytGC7f/axon/native observation fit unchanged,
after it has been frozen. Do not fit any parameter on these animals. Reuse all
main display hypotheses and their exact existing image-driven network outputs.
For each fly, compare magnitude-based translation/yaw epoch selectivity using
the main method and all raw signed epoch contrasts. The published main index
was already known; these newly scored individual animals test transfer rather
than discovery of the preference. Source stimulus frames remain approximate.

Retain the main bIPS property rule: at least three paired animals, observed
95% fly-bootstrap interval wholly positive, primary model mean within it and
positive model sign under every declared display hypothesis. Use 5,000
resamples with fixed seed 202610279301. A second, separate unilateral transfer
rule requires at least three animals and the lower 95% CI of equal-fly
zero-response skill above zero (seed 202610279302). Other scores are descriptive.
Both passing would support these limited response properties in new animals,
not physiological validation of DNp15, the full circuit or small-turn detection.

`prepType=walking` identifies the preparation, not an independently verified
continuous walking state, measured speed or unintended turn. No behavioral
state trace is used here. Do not label these as matched small-yaw recordings.
Keep this domain transfer distinct from the main immobilized-fly benchmark.
Independently verify source indexing, normalization, filtering, per-fly metrics,
bootstrap intervals and decisions. Preserve all failures. Stop after this
supplement; do not use its outcomes to change any model or observation fit.
