No author contact is required or sent. This is an access/matching limitation, not evidence against the biology.

The candidate graph has zero electrical weights and a functional rather than upstream physiological T4/T5 surrogate. The Pokusaeva study's electrical-coupling effects identify a relevant missing mechanism; this model cannot test them by silently inventing conductances. Archived uLPTCrn and H2rn identity qualifications remain in force. The LPi additions support a specific chemical-opponency hypothesis in inspected dot recordings; their effect on fresh walking classification is a separate computational test.

Physiological-validation status for this milestone: **not established**. Previous known-data calcium comparisons remain limited and mixed. Completing the computational experiment cannot change this status. A concrete next biological step is to retrieve the published HS source archive, verify its display lookup and per-cell controls, and freeze a gain-cancelling preferred/null direction benchmark before extracting targets. DNp15 needs an explicit spike/voltage observation model and suitably matched independent recordings. This work is outside the frozen final small-turn verdict and must be a separately versioned benchmark.
