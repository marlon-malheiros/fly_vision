   numerical-refinement flags and changed decisions from their saved ledgers.

   retrained readouts. No new decoder, cutoff, neural fit, room exclusion or

   Separate published evidence, project numerical results and future hypotheses.
   Existing calcium diagnostics are qualified by their synthetic input clamps,
   reused test flies, unresolved display coordinates and observation models.
