# Reading guide for the initial manuscript

16 November 2026.

The completed pilot is a **limitation and sensitivity result**, not a new
biologically validated method. The manuscript reports what was found even
where it weakens the proposed publication direction.

- `manuscript.md` is the editable, complete text with twelve references.
- `manuscript.tex` reproduces the PDF layout.
- `references.bib` contains the bibliographic records.
- `reference_register.json` records the source links, verified bibliographic
  fields and the scope of each citation. This is a selected literature review,
  not an exhaustive novelty assessment.
- The readable PDF is
  `../../output/pdf/alignment_assessment_manuscript_v1.pdf`.
- Detailed evidence and the original and corrected numerical runs are in
  `../../results/alignment_assessment_pilot_v1`.
- The detailed study report is
  `../../docs/ALIGNMENT_ASSESSMENT_PILOT_REPORT_V1.md`.

The two independent questions have different answers. The archived-image
finding does not survive the wider assumed blur as an exact input equivalence;
it cannot be generalized to the original experiment's full mapping data. The
synthetic example demonstrates fewer incorrect rejections when several
alignments are retained, but also unresolved outcomes and retention of wrong
mechanisms. It does not demonstrate superior mechanism recovery.

The pre-analysis protocol is preserved. Numerical tie handling was amended
after the first independent reviewer found unstable single-fit counts; both
versions and the successful corrected review are retained. The manuscript
discloses that amendment and the concurrent execution of two otherwise
single-process stages. No new experiment, public posting or paper submission
has occurred. Authorship and declarations require investigator review.
