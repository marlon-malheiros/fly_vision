# Optomotor closed-loop prototype V1 — 16 November 2026

Objective: implement a new small, connectome-constrained optomotor circuit and test whether its descending output supports visual course correction in a closed-loop virtual robot. This is a new prototype, not a replay of the collaborator's omitted runtime.

Anatomy: derive individually identified central neurons and their chemical edges from official MaleCNS v1.0 tables. Preserve asymmetric connectivity. Use T4/T5 horizontal ON/OFF motion populations as visual inputs; their sensory embedding is a functional modeling assumption. Actual synapse counts constrain relative coupling; transmitter signs, rate dynamics, normalization and time constants remain model assumptions. Missing electrical conductances will not be fabricated. Record boundaries and omitted input coverage.

Sensor and embodiment: render a textured planar arena into an equatorial panoramic camera. The controller receives camera-derived motion through the circuit and reads only descending left/right activity. Pose, true yaw rate, disturbance schedule and scene identity are simulator/evaluator state and are not controller inputs. Steering changes pose and the next camera observation. This simplified virtual robot is not a biomechanical fly.

Calibration: use separate named calibration seeds and imposed rotation stimuli. Fit only motor sign/gain and a stationary baseline under a fixed rule, with no neural-weight fitting and no test outcome access. Record every calibration outcome. Failure to obtain a usable descending signal is a scientific failure, not permission to substitute a truth-based controller.

Evaluation: fresh seed namespace; both yaw directions; zero, low and higher translation speeds; static and straight controls; disturbances with a defined onset/offset. Paired intact, bIPS-silent, open-loop/no-controller, and conventional camera-motion controller. Keep all outcomes. Primary engineering metric: RMS yaw velocity during the disturbance relative to the paired no-controller case. Report translation-induced turning, peak command, heading drift, numerical sensitivity, and failure counts. Pure compensation of motion without heading return is expected for a velocity reflex; no heading-target oracle is supplied.

Advancement: numerical/sensory sign and causal tests must pass; controller must reduce median disturbance RMS in both directions across the prespecified test grid without nonfinite trajectories or commanding rotation on static uniform input. Before any calibration outcome, the straight-travel guard is fixed at median yaw RMS <=2 degrees/s (10% of the imposed 20 degrees/s disturbance), and selected half-step RMS differences must be <=5%. An H2-silent condition is included as a secondary circuit perturbation alongside bIPS silencing. This is a bounded engineering feasibility gate, not a fly physiology or publication gate. Per-case failures and ablation effects remain visible. No circuit-necessity claim follows from a lesion alone.

Source admission fixed the executable V1 architecture before calibration: 20 individually identified HSN/HSE/HSS/HST/H2/PS321/DNp15/LPi12/LPi21 neurons, fed by 6,753 T4a/b and T5a/b cells grouped into eight type/side sensory-drive channels. Retain all measured source-to-core and core-to-core connections, with no synapse-count threshold beyond the official release confidence filter. Normalize by each central neuron's entire source-graph incoming synapse count; omitted partners are zero drive. Retain a ledger of measured central-to-T4/T5 feedback cut by the clamped-source approximation. Recurrence gain 0.9, central time constant 20 ms, tanh signed deviations, ON/OFF correlation delay 30 ms, 256 panoramic pixels, angular [1,2,1]/4 smoothing. These parameters are assumptions, not fitted physiological quantities. The published recurrent interneuron families outside these selected annotations are not silently reconstructed.

Data extraction and model input admission come first. If needed data cannot be retrieved, preserve the exact blocker and retain runnable acquisition code. Do not claim a connectome reconstruction from historical summary plots.

Follow-up biology: compare central-neuron response and perturbation predictions with Erginkaya et al. 2025 and other suitable independently reserved recordings. Physiological validation is outside this initial simulation and remains required for a biological explanation. Physical hardware testing is not performed in this prototype.
